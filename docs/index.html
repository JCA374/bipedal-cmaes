Teaching a network to walk without gradients

A 932-parameter neural network learns to walk in BipedalWalker-v3. No backpropagation, no reinforcement-learning tricks: just CMA-ES sampling, ranking and reshaping a cloud of candidate walkers, on a 4-core laptop. Below is the explainer video, seven lessons to learn the ideas behind it, and the live training run.
