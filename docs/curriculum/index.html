Curriculum: teaching it to walk properly

Every walker so far skips, with one leg always in front. This page follows the attempt to get a fast walker that puts one foot in front of the other, by teaching it in stages: balance, then stepping, then walking, then walking fast. It updates itself about every 30 minutes; the journal at the bottom is my running commentary.

The goal

Measured on 20 tracks the walker never trained on, full episodes, raw reward. All four must hold.

What each stage pays for

The training reward of every stage, exactly as train.py computes it, scored per episode and averaged over 5 tracks. Reported results everywhere else use the real game reward. Each stage also has an expected outcome and the shortcut it might take instead. (Lesson 5 explains the game's own reward.)

The stages

Each stage has its own training reward and a pass test on 20 held-out tracks. It starts from the previous stage's champion, and moves on when it passes, when training plateaus, or at its time cap. Click a stage to see its champion walk and its training curves.
